Top Team

توب تيم · Bible Memory Challenge

A Bible memory and study competition for everyone from nursery age to over 50 — for the whole family. Running since 2012 to gather every family around God’s Word. “God’s Word — Stronger Generations.”

Register your team Open “Memory Journey”
The Top Team vision

God’s Word at the heart of every family

Our goal

To plant one value in every family: gathering around God’s Word — reading the Bible together, and memorizing and studying whole passages of it.

For whom?

For everyone from nursery age to over 50 — the whole family — in churches of every Christian denomination, in schools and in nurseries. We aim to hold it across the whole Arab region.

The idea

The ministry began in 2012 as “Top Ten”, to encourage children, young people and parents to memorize large portions of the Bible and study its characters. The top winners of each region’s exam receive valuable prizes, and every participant receives a gift of Christian books. The ministry runs on donations.

The team

A team of young people driven by the ministry’s vision. Their roles include promotion, designing and distributing the competition’s ads and booklets, booking venues, and running the exams, recitations and marking.

Want the competition in your area?

If you’d like to launch it in a new area or help support it, we’d love to hear from you wherever you are.

The team journey

From registration to celebration

  1. Register

    Register your team or family for your church or school using the form below.

  2. Memorize & study

    Memorize the set passages, study the Bible characters, and practise on the “Memory Journey” app.

  3. The exam

    Each region sits the competition exam — recitation and questions — prepared and marked by the Top Team crew.

  4. Celebration

    Valuable prizes for the top winners, and a gift of Christian books for every participant.

FAQ

Before you register

Who can take part?

The whole family — from nursery age to over 50 — from churches of every denomination, schools and nurseries. Register and the Top Team crew will contact you with the season details.

Does every member need the app?

The app is the easiest way to practice and track progress; our team will walk you through it after you register.

How do I find out about the next season?

Follow our Facebook page, or register your team now and we’ll tell you the dates first.
