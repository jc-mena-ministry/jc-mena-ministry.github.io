Top Team

توب تيم · Bible Memory Challenge

A Bible memory competition for all ages: teams from churches and schools compete to memorize God’s Word, combining team spirit with the fun of a challenge. Running since 2012, with seasons in several countries across the Middle East. “God’s Word — Stronger Generations.”

Register your team Open “Memory Journey”
The team journey

From registration to competition day

  1. Register your team

    The team leader registers the team for their church or school using the form below.

  2. Memory plan

    The team receives the verses and a clear memorization plan.

  3. Daily practice

    Every member practices and tracks progress on the “Memory Journey” app.

  4. Competition day

    Teams gather for a final day celebrating the Word and those who memorized it.

The “Memory Journey” app

The official practice app: verse levels, progress tracking and a daily personal challenge.

FAQ

Before you register

Who can take part?

It’s for all ages — children, youth and adults — from churches and schools across the Middle East. Register your team and the Top Team crew will contact you with the full season details.

Does every member need the app?

The app is the easiest way to practice and track progress; our team will walk you through it after you register.

How do I find out about the next season?

Follow our Facebook page, or register your team now and we’ll tell you the dates first.
