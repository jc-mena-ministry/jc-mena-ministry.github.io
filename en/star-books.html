The Star Books vision

Why Star Books?

Why?

The fast pace of life and the grip of social media on the best hours of our day make it hard — especially for young people — to find time to read. That’s where Star Books began: a youth ministry that helps solve this problem.

For whom?

For young people aged 16 to 35 — and for anyone who has no time to read.

What we offer

Animated reviews of carefully chosen books in three tracks:

  • Spiritual: Christian principles and values that meet their needs — e.g. The Purpose Driven Life by Rick Warren.
  • Emotional: skills for handling deep emotional needs — e.g. Emotionally Healthy Spirituality by Peter Scazzero.
  • Life skills: skills for life and service in the Kingdom — e.g. The 15 Invaluable Laws of Growth by John Maxwell.

The team

A group of young Christians passionate about bringing this ministry to their peers. Their roles range from summarizing and voice recording to designing the animated videos and ads.

For leaders & teachers

How to use Star Books in your group or class

  1. Pick a video

    Choose a book that fits your topic or your group’s needs.

  2. Watch together

    The videos are short — perfect to open a meeting or a class.

  3. Discuss

    Ask: what’s the main idea? What does the Bible say about it? How will we live it this week?

  4. Encourage reading

    Suggest reading the whole book, or one chapter, and follow up together.
