About us

JC — one home for programs that serve the church in the Middle East

“Jesus Can” — that is what we believe and build on. JC is a Christian organization that works with churches and schools to offer practical programs, in the language and tools of this generation, helping young people read, memorize and live God’s Word, and equipping leaders to carry the ministry to the next generation.

Our mission

To place God’s Word in the heart of every young person, through creative programs that fit their world and serve the local church.

Our vision

A generation in the Middle East that knows and loves the Bible, and leads its church and community with faithfulness and strength.

Our partnership

We don’t replace the church or the school — we work alongside them: we provide content, training and tools, and local leaders lead.
