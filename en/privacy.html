Privacy policy

We respect your privacy and the privacy of the young people and children we serve. This page explains simply what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

What we collect

Only what you type into the website forms: name, church or school, city and country, a contact method (WhatsApp or email) and any message you write. The site does not ask for photos or personal details of individual team members.

How we use it

Only to reply to you and organize the program you asked about: confirming a team registration, following up on an Idrak application, or answering your message. We never sell or share your data, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Data is stored in a protected database (Google Firebase) and can only be read by the JC admin account. Other visitors and competitors cannot see any of it.

Children and minors

Top Team registrations are made by the team leader or responsible servant, with the agreement of the parents of members under 18. We do not publish children’s names or photos on the site; the hall of fame lists team and church names only.

Photos

Any photos we publish from our activities are shared with the consent of those responsible. If you see a photo of you or your child that you’d like removed, write to us and we’ll take it down.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to correct or delete your data at any time by emailing jc.mena.ministry@gmail.com.

Third-party services

The site uses Google Fonts, YouTube (for Star Books videos) and Google Firebase (for forms). These services have their own privacy policies.
