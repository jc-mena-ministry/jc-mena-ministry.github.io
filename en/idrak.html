Idrak

إدراك ٢٦ · Young Leaders Program · Ages 20–40

A program that equips young leaders aged 20 to 40, combining spiritual formation with practical leadership skills, so they serve their churches and communities with maturity and impact.

Apply now The “Manager & Leader” book
What the program offers

Three tracks that shape a leader

Spiritual formation

Christian character, life with God, and leadership as service rather than power.

Leadership & management skills

Vision, team building, communication, decision-making and running a ministry.

Hands-on practice

A ministry project in your church, with mentoring and follow-up from experienced leaders.

The program book

Manager & Leader

مدير قائد · Idrak 26

Management and leadership from a scientific perspective with biblical application — for young leaders. “What we recommend team leaders know before their team members do.”

  • Connects modern management science with biblical principles.
  • Written for team and ministry leaders in the church.
  • The core reference for Idrak sessions.
Who can apply?

This program is for you if…

You’re 20 to 40

A young man or woman in a season of building and launching.

You serve, or want to

A servant or team leader in your church, or you sense a call to lead.

You’re committed

Ready to attend the sessions and apply what you learn in your ministry.
